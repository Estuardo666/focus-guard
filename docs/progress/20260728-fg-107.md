## Security, UX, and recovery implications

Installation is administrator-only and stages the independent recovery utility
with the service. ACLs grant SYSTEM and Administrators full control over
machine state, and LocalService modify access; binaries are readable/executable
by SYSTEM, Administrators, and LocalService. The installer snapshots prior
binaries and ACLs, stops an existing service only after backup, and restores
files, permissions, registration, and running state after failure. Uninstall
explains the exact recovery action and does not bypass a finite delay or destroy
policy evidence.
